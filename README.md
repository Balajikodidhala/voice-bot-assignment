AI Voice Bot Assignment

This project contains two AI voice agents built using Sarvam AI Voice Agents. The bots are designed to support customer conversations for loan settlement and car insurance renewal.

1. Personal Loan Settlement Bot

The Personal Loan Settlement Bot is designed to assist customers with pending loan payments and settlement-related questions.

Key features

- Greets customers and explains the purpose of the call.
- Follows the configured customer-verification process.
- Understands payment difficulties and settlement concerns.
- Guides customers through approved payment or follow-up steps.
- Handles objections politely and offers escalation for disputes or complex cases.
- Avoids promising unauthorized discounts or settlement terms.

2. Car Insurance Renewal Bot

The Car Insurance Renewal Bot is designed to help customers with upcoming insurance renewals.

Key features

- Greets customers and explains the renewal purpose.
- Responds to questions about the renewal process.
- Handles objections about high premiums, discounts, and comparing other plans.
- Provides policy information only when it is verified.
- Offers human assistance when information is unavailable.
- Respects customer decisions and requests to end the call.

3. Testing Approach

Both agents are tested using customer-style conversations to evaluate their responses and handling of common objections.

Personal Loan Settlement Bot

- Test scenarios include payment difficulties, requests for settlement discounts, and disputes.
- Responses are checked for clarity, politeness, verification, and appropriate escalation.

Car Insurance Renewal Bot

- Test scenarios include renewal requests, expensive premiums, discount requests, and comparing alternative plans.
- Responses are checked for accuracy, natural conversation, and respect for customer decisions.

5. Design and Safety Principles

- Keep conversations clear, polite, and customer-friendly.
- Do not invent financial, settlement, or insurance information.
- Do not promise unauthorized discounts or terms.
- Protect customer information.
- Respect requests to stop the conversation.
- Escalate issues that require human support.
